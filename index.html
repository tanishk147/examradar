// the problem

The syllabus is huge. The time before exams isn't.

Every student knows past papers are the best guide, and seniors hand down the same advice every year. But going through ten years of scanned PDFs by hand is the last thing you have time for during exam week.

01

Past papers are a mess

Blurry scans, WhatsApp forwards and random Drive folders. Nothing is sorted by topic, and half of it is hard to read.

02

Patterns are invisible

Some topics show up every year for 10+ marks. Others haven't appeared in five years. You can't see that without counting.

03

Planning is guesswork

With three exams in five days, students end up spending equal time on everything instead of what actually carries marks.

// how it works

From a folder of PDFs to a plan in minutes.

1

Upload papers

Drop in scanned PDFs or phone photos of past papers. Add your syllabus if you have it.

2

Every question is extracted, split into parts, and tagged by unit, topic, marks and question type.

// faq

Questions.

Does ExamRadar guarantee what will be on my exam?

No. ExamRadar shows patterns from past papers and ranks topics by how often and how heavily they've appeared. It helps you prioritise. It isn't a leak, and it doesn't replace studying the full syllabus.

Which universities and courses are supported?

Any course with past papers. If your university reuses a similar paper format each year, ExamRadar works especially well. We're starting with engineering and science courses.

Where do the past papers come from?

From students. You upload papers you already have, such as ones from your library, seniors or department. Shared libraries only include papers students choose to share.

Is it free?

Early access is free. We plan to keep a generous free tier for students.
